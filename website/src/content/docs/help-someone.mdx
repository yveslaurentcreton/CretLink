## Who does what

<div class="lanes not-content">
  <div class="lane tech">
    <h3>You</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Start CretLink and choose <strong>Give help</strong>.</li>
      <li>Read the six digits aloud when they appear, and confirm that they match.</li>
      <li>Ask your AI agent to do the work, and watch what it does.</li>
      <li>Stop the session when the work is done.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="lane cust">
    <h3>Your customer</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Starts <code>CretLink-Support.exe</code> and answers <strong>Yes</strong> when Windows asks.</li>
      <li>Picks you from the list and checks the six digits you read.</li>
      <li>Reads what full control means and allows it.</li>
      <li>Watches, and can press <strong>Stop now</strong> at any moment.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>

## Put your AI agent to work

Once the customer has allowed control, the agent you connected sees CretLink's remote tools. Ask it in your own words, for example:

> Use CretLink to find out why the Wi-Fi adapter on the customer's computer reports an error. Tell me what you find before you change anything.

Three habits make a session go well:

- **Let it look first.** Every answer names the computer it came from, and the agent can ask for the computer's details and whether the session has administrator rights. A good first request is to ask where it is.
- **Ask for a purpose.** The agent can state in a few words why it runs something. Your customer reads that purpose next to the real command.
- **Read the results.** A failed command is reported as failed, a cancelled one as cancelled, and one that got lost as unknown. Nothing is reported as done when it is not.

Files go both ways: the agent sends a file to the customer's computer and fetches one from it, and CretLink checks that every copy is identical to the original. A file that leaves the customer's computer is marked as such on both screens.

## When you are done

Press **Stop session**, or let your customer press **Stop now**. CretLink cleans up on their computer and both of you get a report. On your side the session stays under **Sessions**, with its audit trail.
