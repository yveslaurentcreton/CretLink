## What holds in every session

- **No access before consent.** Until the customer allows control at step 4, nothing is shared and nothing runs on their computer.
- **The code is the check.** Six equal digits on both screens mean that the two programs talk to each other directly, with nobody in between.
- **The connection code grants nothing.** It only says where the technician's computer is. Typing it starts steps 3 and 4 like any other session.
- **Stopping needs nobody's help.** Each person stops alone, and the other cannot prevent it.
- **Nothing stays silently.** What CretLink could not remove is named in the report. It is never left out.
